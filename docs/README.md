# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

## Overview

OctoAcme uses a structured, customer-focused approach to project management that emphasizes iterative delivery, clear ownership, and data-informed decisions. This documentation captures our proven processes, roles, and artifacts used across all cross-functional projects that deliver product features, services, or integrations.

## Core Principles

- **Customer-first:** Prioritize customer value and usability in all decisions
- **Iterative delivery:** Deliver small, testable increments to gather feedback early and often
- **Clear ownership:** Each project has a named Project Manager (PM) and Product Lead responsible for outcomes
- **Data-informed decisions:** Measure impact and iterate based on evidence and metrics
- **Psychological safety:** Encourage feedback, learning, and continuous improvement across all teams

## OctoAcme Project Management Processes

OctoAcme executes projects through a defined lifecycle spanning from initiation through retrospectives. Our approach combines structured planning with flexibility to adapt based on learnings. Key workflows include:

- **Daily standups** (15 min) focused on progress, blockers, and dependencies
- **Weekly delivery syncs** to review progress, updates, and flagged risks
- **Regular demos and reviews** at the end of each sprint or milestone
- **Risk management** with escalation paths from team-level triage through sponsor involvement
- **Quality gates** including automated testing, security scanning, and manual QA for acceptance

Across all stages, we maintain clear communication channels, documented decision-making, and consistent processes. Roles include Project Managers who coordinate delivery and timelines, Product Managers who define outcomes and prioritize backlog, and Developers who implement features while collaborating on design and quality.

## Key Artifacts

Across all OctoAcme projects, you'll work with these core artifacts:

- **Project Charter / One-pager** — Problem statement, goals, success metrics, stakeholders, timeline
- **Roadmap and Release Plan** — High-level timeline with milestones and dependencies
- **Sprint/Iteration Backlog** — Prioritized list of work items with acceptance criteria and estimates
- **Risk Register** — Tracked risks with impact, likelihood, mitigation plans, and status
- **Definition of Done (DoD)** — Team agreement on quality and readiness before items are considered complete
- **Retrospective notes and action items** — Learnings and improvements with clear owners and due dates

## Communication Cadence

- **Weekly sync** between PM + Product Manager
- **Twice-weekly standups** for delivery team (or as agreed)
- **Monthly stakeholder updates** with status and key decisions
- **Ad-hoc escalations** as needed for blockers or risks
